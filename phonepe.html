Project Overview

This project presents an interactive analytical look at multi-channel transactional telemetry mimicking consumer interactions on the PhonePe application interface. By organizing large raw transactional rows into clear relational tracking frames via Power BI, the dashboard isolates key consumer demand movements, tracking critical conversions from raw product lines down to targeted user checkout bottlenecks.

Business Problem

Fintech platforms scale dynamically across multiple service domains simultaneously. Without continuous automated tracking models across functional verticals—such as insurance updates, merchant lines, and simple account-to-account operations—identifying systemic user checkout drops, bank system outages, or cyclical transaction declines becomes impossible at an enterprise scale.

Key Dashboard Insights

  • Core Failure Profiles: Checkout logs show the largest failure factors are caused by Server Errors (~33.76%) and Wrong PIN validation entries (~27.61%).
  • Seasonal Spending Peaks: Platform volume trends show an annual spending peak during July (304M), with a notable dip in February (279M).
  • Dominant Sector Share: Loan provisions make up over 75% of the total recorded transaction amount on the dashboard.
  • Stable Success Margins: Across multiple categories, the general success threshold remains stable at ~95.9%.

Strategic Recommendations

  • Optimize Bank Gateways: Address Server Errors (33.76% of failures) by implementing automated fallback routing to healthier bank partner nodes.
  • Enhance UI/UX for Errors: Introduce inline prompt cues for "Wrong PIN" states to reduce repeated failures.
  • Capitalize on High-Volume Months: Schedule infrastructure maintenance during low-activity periods like February, and plan promotional campaigns during peak months like July.
  • Cross-Sell High-Margin Services: Leverage high-traffic areas like P2P Money Transfers to promote underutilized options like Insurance.
