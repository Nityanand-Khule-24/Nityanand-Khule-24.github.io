Project Overview

Customer reviews are one of the most valuable sources of feedback for e-commerce businesses. This project leverages Natural Language Processing (NLP) techniques to analyze Amazon product reviews and classify customer sentiment into Positive, Neutral, and Negative categories. The results are presented through an interactive Power BI dashboard that enables stakeholders to understand customer satisfaction trends and product performance.

Business Problem

Amazon receives thousands of customer reviews every day. Analyzing this feedback manually is inefficient and time-consuming. Businesses need automated solutions capable of identifying customer sentiment, measuring satisfaction levels, and detecting recurring product issues at scale.

Project Objectives

Analyze large-scale Amazon review data sets efficiently.

Classify reviews dynamically into distinct customer sentiments.

Measure exact satisfaction thresholds using structural star ratings.

Identify cyclical customer feedback anomalies and trends.

Build data-rich, low-latency Power BI tracking dashboards.

Generate reliable, data-backed operational business recommendations.

Key Insights

  • Over 90% of customer reviews recorded were positive.
  • Products with 5-star ratings explicitly drove the highest volume thresholds.
  • Negative feedback profiles represented an edge margin of only 7.72%.
  • Customer satisfaction parameters remained highly consistent universally.
  • Proven high correlation factors between structural star indexes and string sentiment pools.
